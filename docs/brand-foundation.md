# HA Brand Foundation

## Purpose

Himalayan Argali exists to help people live healthier, more active lives through thoughtfully selected apparel, nourishment, equipment, and experiences.

## Positioning

HA is a Himalayan-rooted active-lifestyle ecosystem—not only an apparel label and not only a sports retailer. It brings together what people wear, consume, use, and experience while pursuing an active life.

## Brand architecture

### Wear

Technical apparel, everyday activewear, teamwear, accessories, and future HA collections.

### Nourish

Honey, sea buckthorn products, healthy foods, sports nutrition, recovery products, and curated Nurture Boxes.

Nurture Boxes may serve different communities and stages, including children, Cubs, Explorers, Pathfinders, and Guides. Product claims must remain accurate, responsible, and compliant with applicable regulations.

### Equip

Equipment and accessories for ice skating, short-track speed skating, long-track speed skating, figure skating, ice hockey, inline skating, cycling, pickleball, and related sports.

### Explore

Camps, coaching, workshops, events, memberships, expeditions, and community experiences.

## Brand character

HA should feel authentic, energetic, grounded, adventurous, trustworthy, inclusive, and purposeful.

## Initial priorities

1. Make the existing Shopify store coherent around the four pillars.
2. Establish a consistent visual and verbal identity.
3. Build a reliable product catalogue structure.
4. Launch focused hero products before broad expansion.
5. Document suppliers, provenance, safety, and product claims.
6. Create a safe Shopify development workflow.

## Decision rule

Every new product, page, campaign, or feature should answer:

> Does this help people wear well, nourish well, equip well, or explore well?

If not, it needs a clear strategic reason to belong in HA.
