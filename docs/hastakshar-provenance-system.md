# Project हस्ताक्षर — Manufacturing Provenance System
*Himalayan Argali · Brand Master Module · drafted 14 July 2026*

हस्ताक्षर (hastākṣar) — "signature."

---

## 0. Definition

Project हस्ताक्षर is the manufacturing provenance system for Himalayan Argali. Every crafted good is permanently tied to the person who made it and to its place in the Argali Register.

It is not an apparel sub-brand. It is a system that records origin, maker, and life history for anything HA makes by hand.

---

## 1. Position in the ecosystem

Two pillars of one philosophy:

- **Project Adopt-a-Queen** — living provenance (bees and honey). Proves the origin of what the mountain gives.
- **Project हस्ताक्षर** — manufacturing provenance (apparel and crafted goods). Proves the origin of what is made by hand.

```
HIMALAYAN ARGALI
├── Project Adopt-a-Queen        Living provenance (bees & honey)
├── Project हस्ताक्षर             Manufacturing provenance (apparel & crafted goods)
├── On Thin Ice                  Expedition-inspired apparel collection
├── The Argali Register          Permanent archive of products
├── Apparel Division             Performance · Lifestyle · Outerwear · Accessories
└── Brand Bible                  Design system · Manufacturing standards ·
                                 Creative direction · Typography · Colour ·
                                 Packaging · Photography
```

हस्ताक्षर feeds records *into* The Argali Register. The Register is the archive; हस्ताक्षर is the act of signing a piece into it.

---

## 2. Scope

Covers all hand-crafted HA goods. The philosophy must survive catalogue growth, so the system is defined for the category, not the garment:

- Apparel (first application)
- Leather goods
- Ice axes and metal tools
- Wooden products
- Handmade knives (if ever)
- Limited-edition gear

---

## 3. Register numbering

Manufacturing provenance is kept **separate** from honey. Honey carries biological provenance and its own register; garments carry manufacturing provenance. Mixing jars and garments into one sequence makes the archive unreadable in twenty years.

Garment format:

```
HA-G000001
HA-G000002
HA-G000003
```

Devanagari numeral forms may be shown on-product:

```
वस्त्र ०००००१
G-०००००१
```

Devanagari numerals: ० १ २ ३ ४ ५ ६ ७ ८ ९

Future categories take their own prefix letter under the same `HA-` root (e.g. leather `HA-L`, tools `HA-T`), so each category stays a legible sub-sequence.

---

## 4. Maker attribution

The strongest element, extended for durability across factories where responsibilities differ (some stitch, some assemble, some finish, some inspect):

```
crafted by
anita
```

"Crafted by" — not "stitched by." Durable across every production role.

```
क्रमांक
०००१२८
```

(क्रमांक — kramāṅk, "serial number.")

---

## 6. Construction standards — हस्ताक्षर pieces only

- Woven neck label (not printed)
- Embroidered chest crest
- Embroidered OTI crest
- **No printed chest logos**

Embroidery is a permanence signal — it reinforces that the piece is built to last, not to sell a season.

---

## 7. Digital register — the passport page

Every register number has its own page.

```
/hastakshar/000128
```

Page contents:

```
Garment
  Alpine Force Polo
  Drop 02

Maker
  Anita
  Tiruppur

Fabric
  100% Recycled Poly Knit
  180 GSM
  Moisture Wicking

Production date
  12 July 2026

Repairs
  None            (or)   Repaired · 2029

Ownership
  Private by default — shown only if the owner opts in
```

This is a digital passport, not a marketing page. Same documentary tone as the physical label.

---

## 8. Life history

The record accrues over the life of the piece:

```
Registered
   ↓
Purchased
   ↓
Repaired
   ↓
Repaired
   ↓
Inherited
   ↓
Still Active
```

The garment has a life. This is the point of difference from a generic QR code.

---

## 9. QR code

Included quietly. Not visible outside the garment — inside the neck label. Scanning opens the register page directly:

```
Register ०००१२८
```

No app. No login. Just the page.

---

## 10. Drops

Named as expedition journal entries, not fashion seasons:

```
Winter Solstice
First Melt
High Pass
Monsoon Pause
Snow Line
Long Night
```

List is open — expandable as the expedition calendar grows.

---

## 11. Scarcity — formal HA rule

> **Scarcity is evidenced, not announced.**

The register number is the evidence. There are no countdowns, no "only N left" banners. The archive itself communicates rarity.

## 14. Why it matters

Most outdoor brands sell products. This gives Himalayan Argali products that are documented, traceable, and designed to have a long life — a consistent philosophy across honey and craft, rather than conventional marketing scarcity or storytelling.
