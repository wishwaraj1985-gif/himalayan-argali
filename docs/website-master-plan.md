# HA Website Master Plan v0.1

**Status:** Draft for founder review  
**Scope:** `www.himalayanargali.shop`  
**Implementation:** No Shopify theme changes until this plan is approved.

## 1. Objective

Turn the existing Shopify storefront into a coherent Himalayan Argali ecosystem that helps customers understand the brand, find the right product, trust the purchase, and discover the next relevant part of HA.

## 2. Experience principles

- Preserve the existing Himalayan Argali brand identity and approved photography.
- Make the product system understandable before making it visually elaborate.
- Lead with real products, real specifications, and clear provenance.
- Separate inspiration from purchasing decisions.
- Make specialist equipment feel credible and easy to compare.
- Avoid artificial urgency, unsupported health claims, and confusing category names.

## 3. Proposed information architecture

### Primary navigation

- **Wear**
- **Nourish**
- **Equip**
- **Explore**
- **About HA**

### Secondary navigation and utilities

- Search
- Account
- Cart
- Contact and support
- Shipping, returns, and product-care information

### Taxonomy note

The brand materials also use the worlds **On Ice**, **On Wheels**, **Nurture**, and **Experience**. These should remain a merchandising or storytelling layer until the founder approves how they map to the four primary navigation pillars.

## 4. Homepage blueprint

1. **Brand introduction** — what Himalayan Argali is and who it serves.
2. **Four pathways** — Wear, Nourish, Equip, Explore.
3. **Current hero products** — beginning with Honey, Apparel, short-track boots, and inline boots.
4. **Proof and provenance** — materials, makers, origins, and product expertise.
5. **Featured stories** — sport, health, Himalayan connection, or community.
6. **Explore next** — cross-category discovery without forcing unrelated products.
7. **Footer trust layer** — support, policies, contact, and social/community links.

## 5. Collection page blueprint

Each collection should include:

- A concise purpose statement.
- Useful filters and sorting.
- Consistent product cards using existing approved imagery.
- Clear product type, key specification, and price.
- Guidance for choosing between products.
- Related collections and support links.

### First collection priorities

- HA Nurture Honey
- HA Apparel
- Short-track Ice Skating Boots
- Inline Skating Boots

## 6. Product page blueprint

Every product page should answer:

- What is it?
- Who is it for?
- What makes it different?
- What are the exact specifications?
- What sizes, variants, or configurations exist?
- What is included?
- How does it fit, perform, or get used?
- Where did it come from?
- How is it shipped, returned, and supported?

Product pages should reuse existing Shopify photography wherever it is accurate and approved. Missing information should be flagged rather than invented.

## 7. First product-page applications

### Honey

Use the existing active variants and photography. Improve naming consistency, flavour descriptions, provenance information, pack-size clarity, and related-product discovery.

### Apparel

Use existing HA product imagery and line concepts. Separate confirmed products from design concepts and supplier-only references.

### Short-track boots

Separate boot models, blades, and complete packages. Add athlete-level guidance, sizing, compatibility, and support information.

### Inline boots

Separate complete skates, frames, wheels, and accessories. Correct current category assignments before redesigning the collection.

## 8. Checkout and trust journey

Before publication, verify:

- Store mode and checkout availability.
- Shipping and returns.
- Contact and support details.
- Product availability and price accuracy.
- Payment and tax configuration.
- Mobile checkout usability.
- Legal and food-product requirements for Honey and future nutrition products.

## 9. Measurement plan

The first release should establish a baseline for:

- Collection visits and product views.
- Search usage and zero-result searches.
- Add-to-cart rate.
- Checkout initiation and completion.
- Product-page engagement.
- Honey and Apparel cross-discovery.

## 10. Approval gates

Founder approval is required before:

1. Locking the primary navigation taxonomy.
2. Changing the visual brand system.
3. Publishing a new Shopify theme.
4. Making health, nutrition, or performance claims.
5. Deleting or permanently changing existing products or collections.
